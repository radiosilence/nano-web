- **One CI/CD pipeline gated by `Checks`, with jobs chosen by the domains a change touches.** A `changes` job reads the diff and decides, once, whether the run releases; each CI job runs only for its domain, and `Checks` is the single required status. CD runs after `Checks` and ships what CI built: the image is the musl binaries CI built, pushed under the commit and retagged `main` or with the release's version, and the release attaches those same binaries. The glibc and signed macOS builds start alongside the checks on a release only, and crates.io is published after every build, just before the GitHub release that marks the version out.
